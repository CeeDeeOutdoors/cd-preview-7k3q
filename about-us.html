Adam's grandfather Chet, “Gumpie” to all of us, started the Cee Dee name with Cee Dee Flies. He tied for more than 60 years, once supplied Ted Williams with streamers, and loved trolling Lake Winnipesaukee for landlocked salmon with his sons and grandsons.

The Dogs

Adam and Nicole spent their honeymoon hunting rabbits in northern New Hampshire. In 2010, with five children and a couple of vocal beagles, they bought their 10-acre homestead on West Hill in Troy and groomed it into a backyard bird dog training ground for the whole family.

Since then they've added a second location in Pittsburg for dog training and guiding, a third in Hoffman, North Carolina for winter training, and in 2023 a 20' boat for fishing charters.

Adam is a bird dog trainer first. He specializes in grouse dogs, mostly setters, and campaigns them in wild-bird and cover-dog trials.

Good to know

Questions people ask.

Is West Hill Outfitters now Cee Dee Outdoors?

Yes. On December 1, 2026, West Hill Outfitters becomes Cee Dee Outdoors. Same family, same dogs, same phone number: 603-381-2046.

Why the name Cee Dee?

It's the family's name in the outdoors. Adam's grandfather Chet, “Gumpie” to all of us, started it with Cee Dee Flies. Adam's father Paul followed with Cee Dee Tackle and Cee Dee Streamers, and he still ties flies today. Now Adam's son Tanner is joining as a guide, the fourth generation, so the business goes back to that name.

What's on the new logo?

A gold salmon curled around a setter's head, with CEE DEE across it and OUTDOORS below. The fishing and the dogs, in one mark.

What do you do?

Bird dog training first: board-and-train in Troy all year, Grouse Camp in summer, pre-season tune-ups, Winter Training in Hoffman, NC, and field trials. Then guided grouse and woodcock hunts in October, black bear in September, snowshoe hare from Nov 16 to Dec 20, and salmon and trout charters from the third week of April.

Where are you?

Home base is the homestead and training grounds at 54 W Hill Rd in Troy, NH. Hunts are up north in Pittsburg and Berlin. Charters run on Lake Winnipesaukee, Squam Lake, Lake Francis, and First and Second Connecticut Lakes. Winter Training is in Hoffman, NC.

What kind of dogs does Adam train?

Pointing dogs. Most are setters, and Adam specializes in grouse dogs. Call or text him to talk about yours.

Who will I be dealing with?

The family. Adam trains and guides, with help from his son Tanner, the fourth generation. Adam and Nicole Dubriske have run the business since 2010.

Did the phone number or email change?

No. Call or text 603-381-2046, and keep using guide@westhilloutfitters.com for now.

Can I talk to past clients?

Yes. References from past clients are available on request. Just ask Adam.

How do I book or ask a question?

Call or text Adam at 603-381-2046, email guide@westhilloutfitters.com, or send a note through the trip planner and he'll get back to you.
