Before you call

Questions grouse hunters ask.

What does a hunt cost?

$500 a day for 1–2 hunters. Kids 12 and under hunt free. Call Adam about more guns. The rate is the same for 2027.

Can I still get a hunt this year?

No. October 2026 is fully booked. Join the 2027 list and Adam will be in touch with dates. If you want to hunt this year, our snowshoe hare hunts behind the beagles run through December 20, 2026.

When do you guide grouse and woodcock?

All of October. We don't guide grouse hunts into November.

Where do we hunt?

The North Country around Pittsburg, NH: mixed hardwoods and softwoods, and cut timber.

How hard is the walking?

Some of the cover is tough going, but logging roads run all through it for easier walking. Every day is built around what you want and what you're able to do.

Can I bring my own dog?

Yes. We'll work your dog into the rotation with ours.

Is lunch included?

Yes. Full days include a field lunch and drinks.

Is lodging included?

No. Most hunters stay in Pittsburg. Ask us and we'll recommend places to stay and eat.

What license do I need?

A New Hampshire hunting license (buy it online). If you're hunting woodcock, register with HIP too.

Who runs the hunts?

Adam and Nicole Dubriske have run the business since 2010. Adam is a bird dog trainer first, and grouse dogs are his specialty. Adam's son Tanner is joining as a guide. He's the fourth generation.

Is this still West Hill Outfitters?

Yes. Same family, same phone number. The name changes to Cee Dee Outdoors on December 1, 2026, as Adam's son Tanner joins as a guide.

How do I get on the 2027 list?

Send the form on this page, or call or text Adam at 603-381-2046. There's no payment to get on the list.
