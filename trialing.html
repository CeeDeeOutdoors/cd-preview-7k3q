Good to know

Questions dog owners ask.

What kind of trials does Adam run dogs in?

Wild bird and cover dog trials. Most of the dogs are setters, and Adam specializes in grouse dogs.

What has Adam won?

A 2022 Grouse Championship. Derby wins with Hifive's Sweet Basil (2025 USCSDA Southeast Championship derby), and in 2026 with Niantic River Molly (US Complete National Derby, 30+ entries) and Niantic River Sage (Southeastern Derby, 20+ dogs), both on the field trial grounds at Hoffman, NC. In fall 2026 Amber, an English setter, won a puppy stake in New Hampshire and a derby in New Brunswick, Canada, where Stella placed third. In 2026 Super Storm Cliff was runner-up at the Northern New England Woodcock Championship and Fireside Sally Forth was runner-up at the New York Grouse Championship. See the full list ↑

Who trains the dogs?

Adam, with help from his son Tanner. Adam trained every dog on this page and handled all of his 2026 trial dogs himself. Adam and Nicole Dubriske have run the business since 2010.

Does Adam run young dogs?

Yes. Puppy and derby stakes are where a young dog starts. In fall 2026 Amber won a puppy stake in New Hampshire and a derby in New Brunswick, Canada, and Stella was third in that derby. Molly and Sage won their derbies at Hoffman, NC the same year. See fall 2026 ↑

Where were the 2026 results?

Molly and Sage won their derbies on the field trial grounds at Hoffman, NC. Cliff was runner-up at the Northern New England Woodcock Championship and won a fall trial in New Brunswick, Canada. Sally Forth was runner-up at the New York Grouse Championship. Amber's wins came in New Hampshire and New Brunswick, and Mooselook Sam had spring wins in Maine.

Can Adam campaign my dog?

Talk to him. Call or text Adam at 603-381-2046 about your dog and the trials you have in mind. For trial dog pricing, call Adam.

My dog will never see a trial. Does this matter to me?

Yes. The things a judge looks for (finding birds, standing steady on point, handling in cover) are the same things that make a grouse dog worth hunting over. Adam trains pointing dogs all year in Troy, NH for $700 a month.

How do I get a young dog ready for a derby?

Birds and miles. Winter Training 2027 runs January 1 to about March 7 in the pines around Hoffman, NC, on a mix of pre-released quail, quail flying out of Johnny houses, some wild coveys, and woodcock when they're in. It's $1,450 per dog with food and birds included, and it's built for puppies and derby-age dogs. Reserve by December 15.

What is Grouse Camp?

Three months on wild grouse and woodcock in northern New Hampshire for $1,950. It ends in early September, ahead of the October 1 opener. Or try the 6-week pre-season tune-up for $1,000.

Can I talk to past clients?

Yes. References from past clients are available on request. Just ask Adam at 603-381-2046.

Is this still West Hill Outfitters?

Yes. Same family, same dogs, same phone number. The name changes to Cee Dee Outdoors on December 1, 2026, as Adam's son Tanner joins as a guide, the fourth generation.

Call or text 603-381-2046, email guide@westhilloutfitters.com, or send the short form below and he'll get back to you.
