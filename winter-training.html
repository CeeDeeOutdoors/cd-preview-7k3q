The winter that matters

A winter in the kennel is a winter lost.

A bird dog learns from birds, not from the calendar. While the coverts back home are frozen, your dog is down south from January into March, working birds every day in the longleaf pines and wiregrass around Hoffman, NC. Day after day on birds builds the nose, drive and manners that carry into next season.

Before you book

Questions owners ask.

What does Winter Training cost?

$1,450 per dog for the whole trip, food and birds included. A $250 deposit holds your spot, then $600 is due January 1 and $600 is due February 1.

What's included in the $1,450?

Food for the whole trip, birds all winter (pre-released quail, quail flying out of Johnny houses, some wild coveys, and woodcock when they're in), Adam with your dog every day (with help from his son Tanner), access to our kennel app with regular updates, and your choice of drop-off: Troy, NH, along the way, or Hoffman, NC.

When does it run?

January 1 to about March 7, 2027. Dates depend on weather.

Where does my dog train, and on what birds?

In the longleaf pines and wiregrass around Hoffman, North Carolina, on a mix of pre-released quail, quail flying out of Johnny houses, some wild coveys, and woodcock when they're in. Open pine country, with steady bird contacts all winter.

Why send my dog south for the winter?

Because birds make bird dogs. While the coverts back home are frozen, your dog is on birds all winter down south, building the nose, drive and manners that carry into next season. As Adam puts it, this training is extremely important in development, especially for young dogs.

Is it right for my dog?

It's built for young dogs (puppies and derby-age dogs that need bird contacts and miles), seasoned hunting dogs that should stay sharp and fit, and trial dogs that need conditioning and birds through the southern season. Not sure? Call Adam and talk it over.

Who handles my dog?

Adam is with your dog every day, with help from his son Tanner. Adam is a bird dog trainer first and has been training bird dogs for 15 years from Troy, New Hampshire.

Has Adam won field trials?

Yes. Adam trained and handled Niantic River Molly, who won the 2026 US Complete National Derby (30+ entries), and Niantic River Sage, who won the 2026 Southeastern Derby (20+ dogs), on the Hoffman, NC field trial grounds, and Amber, who won a derby in New Brunswick, Canada. He runs Winter Training himself. See all results.

How does my dog get to North Carolina?

Your choice. Drop off in Troy, NH, meet us along the way, or bring your dog to Hoffman, NC. Adam will set it up with you.

Will I get updates during the trip?

Yes. You'll get access to our kennel app, with regular updates on your dog all winter.

When do I need to reserve?

By December 15, 2026. Space is limited.

How do I pay the deposit?

Send the form or call Adam at 603-381-2046. After he confirms your spot, the $250 deposit can be paid by Venmo to @Adam-Dubriske.

What if my plans change?

The $250 deposit is non-refundable. If your dog is injured or sick and can't make the trip, it carries over to another dog this season or to Winter Training 2028. If you cancel for any other reason before Dec 15, it carries over too. After Dec 15, cancellations for other reasons forfeit the deposit.

Can my dog keep training after the winter?

Yes. Back in Troy, NH, monthly training is $700 a month. Grouse Camp ($1,950) puts your dog on wild grouse and woodcock in northern New Hampshire from late May or early June to early September, and the 6-week pre-season tune-up is $1,000.

Can I talk to past clients?

Yes. References from past Winter Training clients are available on request. Just ask Adam at 603-381-2046.

Is this still West Hill Outfitters?

Yes. Same family, same dogs, same phone number: 603-381-2046. Adam's son Tanner is joining as a guide, the fourth generation, so the business goes back to the family name, Cee Dee Outdoors, on December 1, 2026.
